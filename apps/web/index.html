How it works

Keep the context. Mask the private details.

Paste text into the local composer, run a scan, then review and copy the protected draft. In this fictional support request, PromptVeil masks the customer ID and email while keeping the damaged-package context and $48 refund.

Original prompt
Draft a friendly reply to a customer whose package arrived damaged. Customer ID: 48372651. Email: maya.patel@example.com. Offer a replacement or a $48 refund.
Protected draft
Draft a friendly reply to a customer whose package arrived damaged. Customer ID: [Customer ID]. Email: [Email]. Offer a replacement or a $48 refund.
